# 6. British Mandate Period
Following World War I, the British Mandate established new political realities in Palestine. This chapter will investigate the complexities of British rule, including the Balfour Declaration and its implications for Jewish immigration. The social and political tensions that arose among various communities will be examined, alongside the roots of the Palestinian national movement during this critical period.
