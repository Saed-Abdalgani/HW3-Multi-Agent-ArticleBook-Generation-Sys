# Historical Empires

Palestine has been ruled by numerous empires throughout its history, each leaving a mark on its culture and society. The Assyrians, Babylonians, Persians, and Greeks all controlled the region at different points in time.

The Roman Empire's influence was particularly profound, culminating in the establishment of Jerusalem as a major urban center. The spread of Christianity during this period transformed Palestine into a focal point for Christian pilgrimage.
