# BiDi Demonstration Chapter

This chapter serves as a demonstration of BiDi (Bidirectional) text handling in Markdown. Here, we will explore how languages such as Hebrew can coexist with English in written form.

For example, the Hebrew phrase: 'שלום עולם' (Shalom Olam - Hello World) illustrates the integration of Hebrew within an English context. Understanding BiDi text is crucial for accurately representing the diverse linguistic landscape of Palestine.
