# Modern Era

The modern era in Palestine is characterized by significant political upheaval and conflict. The late 19th and early 20th centuries saw the rise of nationalist movements, which were affected by external influences such as colonialism.

The Balfour Declaration of 1917 marked a turning point, as it promised a national home for the Jewish people in Palestine, leading to increased tensions between Jewish and Arab populations.

As the 20th century progressed, the establishment of the State of Israel in 1948 resulted in the displacement of many Palestinians, a pivotal moment that continues to resonate today. The modern era is crucial for understanding the ongoing struggle for Palestinian rights and statehood.
